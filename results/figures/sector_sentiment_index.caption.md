# Equity-sector headline sentiment over time

## Note
Daily sector sentiment equal-weights covered ticker-day scores; missing ticker-days are omitted rather than treated as neutral. The chart smooths the daily index with a 21-trading-day rolling mean for readability.

## Sample
2020-01-03 to 2023-12-29

## Units
VADER compound score from -1 to +1.

## Source
FINS5545 hosted project data; calculations by the author.
